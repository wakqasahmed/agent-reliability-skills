# Sources

Normative, externally verifiable guidance uses stable source IDs in square brackets. Recheck
sources after material changes and at least within the window below. Repository mechanics and
evidence observed during a measurement do not need citations.

Freshness window: 180 days.

## SRC-CMA-AI-AGENTS

- Publisher: Competition and Markets Authority (UK)
- Official URL: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/complying-with-consumer-law-when-using-ai-agents
- Supported claim: A business that uses an AI agent remains responsible for what that agent does, including when the agent acts unlawfully.
- Specification version: Guidance published 9 March 2026
- Last verified: 2026-09-11

## SRC-SRE-BURN-RATE

- Publisher: Google (Site Reliability Engineering Workbook, chapter 5, Alerting on SLOs)
- Official URL: https://sre.google/workbook/alerting-on-slos/
- Supported claim: Burn rate is how fast, relative to the objective, a service consumes its error budget; multiwindow multi-burn-rate alerting turns budget consumption into an actionable alert instead of alerting on individual failures.
- Specification version: SRE Workbook, 1st edition
- Last verified: 2026-09-11

## SRC-SR-26-2

- Publisher: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, and Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation
- Official URL: https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm
- Supported claim: Effective challenge of a model is performed by individuals with sufficient independence to maintain objectivity, as well as the organizational standing and influence to effect any change. Supersedes and replaces SR 11-7 (2011).
- Specification version: SR 26-2, issued 17 April 2026
- Last verified: 2026-09-11
